Privacy Policy

Effective: November 25, 2026

Hinata Tomoda (“we”) sets out how the iPhone app Hizuke (“the app”) handles information.

1. Summary

  • The app has no server, no account, no ads and no tracking of you.
  • Photos, thumbnails, file names and where a photo was taken never leave your device.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are sent only if you agree, and you can withdraw at any time in the app.

2. What stays on your device

  • Camera: used only to take photos. Video and photos are developed on this iPhone.
  • Photo library: used only to add the developed photos. The app does not read your library. “Import from Photos” (paid) receives only the photos you pick in the iOS photo picker.
  • Originals: to develop a photo again, the app can keep the original on this iPhone (Settings: All, Recent 100 or None). Originals are never sent off the device and are excluded from iCloud backup; deleting the app deletes them.
  • Photo records, settings and your consent choice are stored on this iPhone.

3. Your choice: usage statistics and crash reports

Choose separately with the switch on the first-run screen or in Settings > Privacy (Share Usage Statistics, Send Crash Reports). Both are off by default and nothing is sent until you agree. Turning one off stops sending at once and deletes the unsent data and the anonymous ID kept on your device.

If you agree, we send:

  • Usage statistics: the app contains a usage-statistics function (PostHog, United States) but it is not enabled in version 1.0. A version that enables it will send only a fixed set of events (codes, counts and yes/no values) and only from people who agreed.
  • Crash reports: the kind of crash and where it happened (function names), app version, iOS version, device model, memory and thermal state and a random per-install ID. Sent to Sentry (United States).

We never send, whether or not you agree: photos and thumbnails, what is in them, file names and paths, location, photo-library identifiers, free-space values, contact details such as your email, or screen recordings.

As part of how the internet works, your device's IP address reaches the recipient; we configure the services not to store or use IP addresses or location. Nothing is used for advertising or for tracking you across other apps and sites.

4. Purpose and retention

Crash reports are used only to investigate bugs and are kept at most 90 days at Sentry (a version that enables usage statistics keeps them at most 1 year at PostHog). We do not sell the data or provide it for advertising.

5. Purchases

Purchases of “All Films” are processed by Apple's App Store. Payment details never reach us. The app decides whether a purchase is valid only from verified App Store transactions on the device.

6. Your rights

We hold no information that identifies you. The random per-install ID is deleted when you turn sending off, and a new one is created if you turn it on again. If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you can withdraw consent at any time (see 3). Questions: contact us below.

7. Children

The app asks for no personal information from anyone, of any age.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and show the revision date. Changes that are less favorable to you, or that add information to be sent, will ask for your consent again in the app.

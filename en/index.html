FAQ

Where are my photos saved?

After developing, a photo is added to the Photos app. Hizuke only adds to your library and never reads it. “Saved” appears only after Photos confirms the save.

My photo says it is waiting and isn't saved.

If permission to add photos is off, photos wait inside the app. Allow it in iOS Settings > Hizuke > Photos; the photos are saved the next time you return to the app.

How is the date decided?

It is made from the time you took the photo. When an imported photo has no date, Hizuke does not invent one. You can choose the order (year-month-day, month-day-year, day-month-year) and whether the time is shown from the date menu on the camera screen.

What does the paid “All Films” include?

Five more films, re-developing kept originals with another film or date format, and developing photos imported from your library. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. You can try the paid films in the viewfinder and the compare sheet before buying.

How do I use my purchase on another iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases under Purchase in the app's Settings.

I'd like a refund.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What are kept originals? I'm worried about storage.

To develop a photo again, Hizuke can keep the original on this iPhone. In Settings > Keep Originals choose All, Recent 100 or None, and use Delete Kept Originals to clear them. Originals never leave the iPhone and are excluded from iCloud backup. Deleting the app deletes them.

Is any data sent anywhere?

Photos, thumbnails, file names and your device location are never sent. Usage statistics and crash reports are sent only if you agree on the first-run screen or in Settings > Privacy, and you can turn them off at any time. See the Privacy Policy for details.
